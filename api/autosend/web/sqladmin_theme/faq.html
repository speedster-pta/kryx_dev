The WhatsApp Business Platform

What is the WhatsApp Business Platform?
The WhatsApp Business Platform (also called the WhatsApp Cloud API) is Meta's messaging infrastructure for businesses that need to send WhatsApp messages at scale, with automation, from software rather than a phone. It's a different product to the free WhatsApp Business app you install on a phone: Kryx connects to the Business Platform on your behalf, so campaigns, reminders and automated replies can be sent from your account rather than someone typing them out one by one.
Do I need my own Meta or WhatsApp Business account, or does Kryx provide one?
You go through Meta's own signup flow (Embedded Signup) from inside Kryx to connect a WhatsApp number to your own Meta Business account, which you own and control. Kryx never owns your WhatsApp number or your Meta account; it connects to them on your behalf so messages can be sent through your account.
Can I use my existing WhatsApp number?
Yes, in most cases. A number already active on the WhatsApp Business app can be migrated to the Business Platform, or connected using Meta's coexistence feature described below. A brand new number can also be registered directly on the Business Platform if you'd rather keep it separate from a number you're already messaging from.
What is a message template, and why does it need approval?
Any message you send to someone who hasn't messaged you first, or any automated message sent outside an active 24-hour customer service window, has to be sent as a pre-approved template. Meta reviews the wording (and any image, document or buttons) before it can be used, which usually takes minutes to a day. Once approved, you can reuse that template with different names, dates or details filled in for each recipient.

Coexistence with the WhatsApp Business app

What is "coexistence"?
Coexistence is a Meta feature that lets the same phone number run the WhatsApp Business app on a phone and the WhatsApp Business Platform (used by Kryx) at the same time, keeping the same contacts and chat history. Without coexistence, connecting a number to the Business Platform stops it from being used in the app on a phone.
Can my team still reply from the app on their phone after connecting to Kryx?
If coexistence is available for your number and turned on during setup, yes: your team keeps chatting 1-to-1 from the app, while Kryx sends campaigns, reminders and automated replies from the same number in the background. If you connect a fresh number instead, there's no app history to preserve and coexistence isn't relevant.
Is coexistence available for South African numbers?
Yes. South Africa was one of the last countries Meta added, but coexistence is now fully available for South African numbers, so your team can keep using the WhatsApp Business app on a phone while Kryx sends through the same number in the background.
Will connecting to Kryx delete my contacts or chat history?
No. Connecting a number, with or without coexistence, doesn't delete anything already on the phone. Coexistence keeps the app's contacts and chat history exactly as they were; migrating a number without coexistence still leaves the phone's local chat history intact, it just stops receiving new messages on that app going forward.

How Meta prices WhatsApp messages

Who actually charges for WhatsApp messages, Kryx or Meta?
Meta sets and charges the per-message rate for messages sent through the WhatsApp Business Platform, billed directly to your own Meta Business account (via the payment method you add there). Kryx's own subscription and add-on pricing is separate from, and additional to, whatever Meta charges you for messages themselves.
How does Meta price messages today?
Since 1 July 2025, Meta charges per individual message delivered, not per 24-hour conversation as it used to. Every message falls into one of four categories: marketing, utility, authentication or service, each with its own rate that varies by country. Marketing and authentication messages are billed every time; utility messages are billed only when sent outside an open customer service window; service messages (your replies inside a chat the customer started) are currently free.
What's a "customer service window", and which of my messages are free?
Whenever a customer messages you, or replies to a template, a 24-hour window opens during which you can reply freely without it counting as a billed template. Within that window: your own free-form replies are service messages (currently free), and a utility template sent in reply is also free. Outside that window, or as the first message to someone who hasn't written to you, only an approved template can be sent, and it's billed at the rate for its category.
Does the price differ by country?
Yes, significantly. Meta publishes a separate rate per category for every country, and rates can differ by several times between markets. Meta's own WhatsApp Business Platform pricing page, linked to your Meta Business account, always has the current rate for your country and category; we'd rather point you there than quote a number here that could be out of date by the time you read it.

24-hour messaging limits

What is the 24-hour messaging limit?
Every WhatsApp number has a limit on how many different customers it can message first (rather than reply to) within a rolling 24-hour period. New numbers start at 250 unique customers a day, and the limit rises through 1,000, 10,000 and 100,000 as Meta sees consistent sending with a good quality rating, eventually removing the cap entirely. This is separate from the 24-hour customer service window described above, which is about when a template is required, not how many people you can message.
What happens if a campaign or reminder hits the limit partway through?
Kryx checks against your number's limit before every send, and if Meta rejects a message because the limit's been reached, that recipient is deferred and automatically retried once the rolling 24-hour window clears, rather than being logged as a failed send. Recipients already messaged earlier in the same campaign aren't affected, only the ones still waiting to be sent.
How do I move up to a higher limit tier?
Meta raises your number's tier automatically based on sending volume and quality rating (how often recipients block, report or engage with your messages), not through anything you configure in Kryx. Sending relevant, well-targeted messages that people don't block keeps the quality rating high, which is what moves a number up to the next tier over time.
Is this the same as my Kryx plan's own message limit?
No, these are two separate limits. Meta's 24-hour messaging limit applies to your WhatsApp number itself, regardless of what sends through it. Your Kryx plan has its own message quota as part of your subscription; reaching that is a billing matter (upgrade your plan or wait for the billing period to roll over), not something that clears itself after 24 hours the way Meta's limit does.

Upcoming service message pricing changes

What is changing on 1 October 2026?
Meta has announced that from 1 October 2026, service messages, your free-form replies inside a chat a customer started, stop being free. Each one will be billed at the same per-message rate Meta already charges for utility messages in that country. Until now, replying to a customer who messaged you first has been free; going forward, ongoing back-and-forth conversations carry a cost per reply.
Does this affect the 72-hour window after someone messages in from a Click-to-WhatsApp ad?
No. Meta has said that entry-point window stays free and unaffected by this change, so a conversation started from a Click-to-WhatsApp ad still opens with a free start, only the ordinary customer service window is affected.
Will this change Kryx's own subscription pricing?
This is a change to what Meta itself charges on your Meta Business account for the messages sent through it, separate from Kryx's own subscription. It doesn't automatically change your Kryx plan or add-on pricing. If it leads us to change anything on our side, we'll email every affected organisation ahead of time rather than changing it without notice.
What should I do to prepare?
Nothing urgent is required before the change lands, but it's worth checking how much of your WhatsApp usage is ongoing back-and-forth replies versus one-off templates, since that's the volume that will start being billed. Meta has said it will publish the exact country rates for service messages by 1 September 2026, on the same pricing page in your Meta Business account referenced above.

Getting started with Kryx

Do I need Planning Center Online to use Kryx?
No. Bulk WhatsApp campaigns and templates are the core Kryx product, available to every organisation. Planning Center Online is an optional module you can switch on later if your organisation uses it, for automated registration confirmations, serving reminders and form replies, but it's never required to get started.
Can I manage multiple branches, campuses or properties from one account?
Yes. Kryx lets you structure your organisation into units, a branch, campus, grade or property, each with its own WhatsApp number, templates and user access, all managed centrally from one Kryx account.
How do I bring in my existing contact list?
Import contacts from a CSV file, an Excel spreadsheet or a Google Sheet, whichever format your existing list is already in.
