Built for real, everyday use cases

Whatever you're sending, Kryx gets it delivered, read and acted on.

Bulk announcements and campaigns

Extend a personalised invite, offer or update to your whole list with images and buttons in one send. Compared to email, your announcement actually gets opened instead of sitting unread in a promotions tab.

Booking confirmations

The moment someone signs up for an event, they get an instant WhatsApp confirmation, not a confirmation email that lands three hours later or gets flagged as spam.

Timely reminders

Anyone with an upcoming appointment, shift or slot gets reminded automatically beforehand. Unlike an SMS, a WhatsApp reminder shows you it was delivered and read, so no more "I never got the text."

Calendar invites
