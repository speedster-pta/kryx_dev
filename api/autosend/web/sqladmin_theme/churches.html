Built specifically for Planning Center Online

Once the Planning Center module is switched on and configured, these run automatically off your existing PCO data — no separate list to maintain.

Registration confirmations

The moment someone registers for an event or service in Planning Center, they get an instant WhatsApp confirmation, not a confirmation email that lands hours later or gets flagged as spam — with a secure payment link included where the event requires one.

Serving team reminders

Volunteers scheduled in Planning Center Services get reminded automatically before their serving slot. A WhatsApp reminder shows you it was delivered and read, so no more "I never got the text."

People-form confirmations

Anyone who submits a Planning Center People form gets an immediate WhatsApp reply confirming it went through, mapped straight from the form to a template of your choice.

iCal event for services and serving

Every serving reminder and Planning Center event is also available as an iCal event, so volunteers and staff can add them to whichever calendar app they already use.

Multi-campus ready

Match Kryx units to your Planning Center campuses, so each campus's registrations, serving teams and forms trigger messages from that campus's own WhatsApp number.
