Kryx Refund and Cancellation Policy

1. General

This policy explains how refunds and cancellations are handled for Kryx, the WhatsApp automation platform provided to Customers.

Unless otherwise required by law or stated in this policy, fees paid to Kryx are non-refundable.

2. Non-refundable fees

Kryx generally does not refund:

  • Subscription fees for a billing period that has already started;
  • Once-off module or integration activation fees;
  • Usage-based charges already incurred, including messages already sent; or
  • Fees where a Customer simply changes their mind or stops using the service without cancelling.

3. When refunds may apply

Kryx may provide a full or partial refund where:

  • Billing error: the Customer was incorrectly or twice charged, or was charged after a valid cancellation;
  • Service not provided: Kryx failed to provide a paid service due to a fault on Kryx's side;
  • Material service failure: a significant or prolonged failure of the core Kryx platform materially prevented the Customer from using a service they paid for; or
  • Required by law: a refund is required under applicable law, including where applicable under the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 ("CPA").

Refund requests must be submitted in writing to Kryx's support contact and should explain the circumstances giving rise to the request.

Approved refunds will normally be made to the original payment method, subject to applicable law and the payment provider's procedures.

4. Cancellation

Customers may cancel their subscription at any time through the account's billing page.

Cancellation:

  • Stops future subscription charges;
  • Takes effect from the end of the current paid billing period, unless otherwise stated or required by law; and
  • Does not ordinarily entitle the Customer to a refund for the current or previous billing periods.

Usage charges incurred before cancellation remain payable.

5. Chargebacks

Customers should contact Kryx first to resolve billing disputes before initiating a chargeback with their bank or card provider.

Kryx may suspend access to an account where an amount remains legitimately due and is subject to an unresolved chargeback or payment dispute.

6. Consumer rights

Nothing in this policy limits any right that a Customer has under applicable law.

Where the CPA applies, Customers may have additional rights, including:

  • A five-business-day cooling-off period for qualifying agreements resulting from direct marketing;
  • Rights relating to the cancellation of qualifying fixed-term agreements; and
  • Remedies where services do not meet the standards required by the CPA.

The application of these rights depends on the Customer and the particular transaction. For example, section 14 of the CPA does not apply to transactions between juristic persons.

Where a Customer believes a statutory right to cancel or obtain a refund applies, they should notify Kryx in writing. Kryx will assess the request in accordance with the applicable law.

7. Changes to this policy

Kryx may update this policy from time to time. The version applicable to a payment will generally be the version in force when that payment was made, unless a later version is required to apply by law.

8. Contact

Refund and cancellation requests should be submitted through Kryx's designated support contact.
