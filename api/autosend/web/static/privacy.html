Kryx Privacy Policy

Kryx ("Kryx", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information processed through the Kryx platform.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the rights and choices available to you.

1. About Kryx

Kryx is a platform that enables organisations to send WhatsApp messages, including bulk, transactional and automated communications, to their contacts.

Where an organisation uses Kryx to communicate with its own contacts, that organisation remains responsible for determining why and how the personal information of its contacts is processed. Kryx acts as a service provider/operator processing information on the organisation's behalf.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Kryx, we may process:

  • Account information: such as your name, email address, organisation details and login credentials.
  • Contact information: such as names and WhatsApp/mobile phone numbers uploaded or supplied by an organisation.
  • Message information: including message content, templates, campaign information and delivery status.
  • Technical information: such as IP addresses, browser information, device information, timestamps and system logs.
  • Usage information: such as information about how users interact with the Kryx platform.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for a specific service and lawfully provided to us.

3. How We Use Information

We may use personal information to:

  • provide and operate the Kryx platform;
  • send WhatsApp messages on behalf of organisations;
  • process message delivery and delivery-status information;
  • authenticate users and secure accounts;
  • provide customer support;
  • monitor, troubleshoot and improve the platform;
  • maintain system records and aggregated usage statistics;
  • comply with legal and regulatory obligations; and
  • prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorised use of the platform.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

4. Information Processed on Behalf of Organisations

Where an organisation uses Kryx to send messages to its contacts, the organisation generally determines the purpose and lawful basis for processing that information.

The organisation is responsible for ensuring that it has the necessary rights, permissions and lawful basis to collect and use its contacts' personal information and to send communications to them.

Kryx processes this information only as necessary to provide the contracted services, subject to the organisation's instructions and applicable law.

5. WhatsApp and Third-Party Services

Kryx uses third-party services, including Meta/WhatsApp infrastructure, to deliver WhatsApp communications.

Information submitted to or processed through those services may also be subject to their respective terms and privacy policies.

Kryx may also use other carefully selected service providers for functions such as hosting, infrastructure, authentication, analytics, email and technical support.

We require service providers that process personal information on our behalf to maintain appropriate confidentiality and security safeguards.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations and protect our legitimate interests.

Where an organisation terminates its use of Kryx, information processed on its behalf will generally be deleted or returned in accordance with the applicable agreement, unless retention is required by law or reasonably necessary for legitimate business purposes.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. International Transfers

Kryx and its service providers may process or store information in countries other than South Africa.

Where personal information is transferred across borders, we will take reasonable steps to ensure that the transfer is conducted in accordance with applicable data-protection laws and that appropriate safeguards are in place.

9. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may have rights relating to their personal information, including the right to:

  • request access to personal information we hold about them;
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • request deletion of information where legally applicable;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
  • lodge a complaint with the relevant data-protection authority.

Where Kryx processes information on behalf of an organisation, requests relating to that organisation's contacts may need to be directed to the organisation first.

10. POPIA

Kryx operates in accordance with applicable South African data-protection requirements, including the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA), to the extent applicable to our activities.

Nothing in this Privacy Policy limits any rights or protections provided to individuals by applicable law.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, legal requirements or privacy practices.

The updated version will be published through the Kryx platform or website, together with its effective date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise a privacy right, please contact Kryx through the contact details provided on the Kryx website.
